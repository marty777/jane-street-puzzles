# Jane Street September 2026 Puzzle

## Description

The [September 2026 puzzle](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/hint-singles-index/), "Hint Singles", is a photo of the cover of an LP propped up against a shelf of records. The cover lists 23 song titles, plus a bonus track by an artist obscured by a price sticker for $19.65. Several unusual boxes - "AS SEVEN ON TV", "NOT SOLID IN STORES" and "STEREO ALP" -  appear along the bottom of the cover.

## Solution

This one's a groaner. Each title is a reference to a real song. Adding one letter to the artist's name, as hinted at by the boxes along the bottom of the cover, produces a terrible pun. Collecting all the additional letters forms a final clue to the puzzle answer.
